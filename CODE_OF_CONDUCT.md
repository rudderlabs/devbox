# Code of conduct

DevBox follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be kind, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment of any kind is not accepted in issues, pull requests, Discussions or any other DevBox space.

Report unacceptable behavior to [devbox@rudderstack.com](mailto:devbox@rudderstack.com). Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential.
